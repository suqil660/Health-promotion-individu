About Health Promotion

Health promotion is the process of helping individuals and communities improve their health and quality of life. It encourages people to practise healthy behaviours, prevent diseases and make better health decisions.

Healthy Lifestyle

A healthy lifestyle includes balanced nutrition, regular physical activity, adequate sleep and good personal hygiene.

Disease Prevention

Disease prevention helps reduce the risk of illness through vaccination, health screening, hygiene and healthy daily habits.

Health Awareness

Health awareness helps individuals understand health risks and encourages them to take action to protect their health.

Healthy Lifestyle Tips

Small changes in daily habits can contribute to better health and well-being.

🥗

Healthy Eating

Eat a balanced diet containing vegetables, fruits, protein and appropriate amounts of carbohydrates.

🏃

Physical Activity

Stay physically active through activities such as walking, jogging, cycling or sports.

💧

Drink Water

Drink enough water throughout the day to help maintain normal body functions and prevent dehydration.

😴

Get Enough Sleep

Having adequate and regular sleep can support physical health, concentration and daily activities.

Disease Prevention

Prevention is an important part of maintaining good health and reducing the risk of disease.

🧼 Personal Hygiene

Wash your hands regularly, maintain personal cleanliness and practise good hygiene habits.

💉 Vaccination

Vaccination helps protect individuals against certain infectious diseases.

🩺 Health Screening

Regular health screening can help identify certain health problems early.

🚭 Avoid Smoking

Avoiding tobacco products can reduce exposure to harmful substances and support better health.
